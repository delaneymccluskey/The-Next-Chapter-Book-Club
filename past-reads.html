Our Past Reads

Our past reads include a mix of different genres, authors, and types of stories. Some books have taken us into fantasy worlds, while others have focused on romance, science fiction, or suspense. Choosing different types of books gives our members a chance to step outside their usual reading preferences and try something new.

This page keeps track of some of the books we have read as a club. It also gives members a place to look back at previous selections and see which books they may want to read if they missed one of our discussions. Whether you are looking for a new favorite or just want to see what our club has been reading, our past reads are a good place to start.
