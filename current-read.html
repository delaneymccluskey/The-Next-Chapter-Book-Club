Our Current Read

The Love Hypothesis

by Ali Hazelwood

The Love Hypothesis is a romance novel that follows Olive Smith, a PhD student who is focused on her research and career. After Olive agrees to pretend to be in a relationship, she unexpectedly becomes involved with Adam Carlsen, a well-known and intimidating professor at her university.

As the story continues, Olive has to figure out how to handle the difference between the fake relationship and her real feelings. The book combines romance, humor, and life as a graduate student while following Olive as she deals with relationships, school, and her future.

Why We Chose This Book

We wanted to choose a book that would be entertaining and give members different things to talk about. The Love Hypothesis includes romance, relationships, school, and career goals, which can lead to different opinions during our book discussions.

Readers who enjoy romance novels and stories with humor may enjoy this month's selection. Members can also use our discussion to compare the characters and their choices throughout the story.

Reading Schedule

Members can read at their own pace, but the following schedule gives everyone a general idea of what to read before our discussion.

  • Week 1: Begin the book and meet the main characters.
  • Week 2: Continue reading and discuss the relationships.
  • Week 3: Finish the book and think about the main themes.
  • Week 4: Book club discussion and favorite moments.

Reading Together

One of the best parts of a book club is being able to share the reading experience with other people. Discussing books together can give readers new ideas and different perspectives on a story.
