About The Next Chapter

The Next Chapter Book Club was created for readers who enjoy discovering new stories and talking about the books they read. We wanted to create a place where reading could be something people enjoy together instead of something they always have to do on their own.

Our club includes readers with different interests and reading habits. Some members read all the time, while others may only have time for a book every once in a while. We think that having different types of readers makes our discussions more interesting because everyone can bring their own perspective.

What Our Club Is Like

Our book club is meant to be relaxed, welcoming, and open to different opinions. We do not expect everyone to feel the same way about a book. Part of the fun is hearing what other people noticed or thought about the story.

  • Friendly book discussions
  • New book and author discoveries
  • Recommendations from other readers
  • Different opinions and perspectives

What We Look For in a Book

When choosing books, we like stories that give members something to talk about. We look for interesting characters, enjoyable stories, and books from different genres. We also like to change things up so that members have opportunities to try books they may not normally choose.

Our goal is not to choose the same type of book every time. We want our reading list to include different stories and give everyone a chance to discover something new.

Why We Read Together

Reading can be a personal activity, but discussing a book with other people can make the experience even more interesting. Talking about characters, storylines, and different interpretations can help readers notice things they may have missed while reading on their own.
